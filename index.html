Frequently Asked Questions

What is Indus AI?

Indus AI is an AI-powered chat assistant that answers questions fluently in dozens of languages. It includes text chat, voice conversations, image generation, and photo-based answers.

Is Indus AI free?

Yes, Indus AI is free to use, with no sign-up required. Creating an account is optional — it's only needed if you want your chat history saved to the cloud.

Is my data safe?

Conversations saved to your account are stored securely and are visible only to you. In guest mode, your data stays only in your own browser.

Which languages are supported?

Indus AI replies fluently in whichever language you write or speak in — including Hindi, English, and most major world and regional languages.

How do I use voice mode?

Tap the microphone or voice button on the chat screen and start speaking. Indus AI will understand you and can reply out loud too.
