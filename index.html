Indus AI kya hai?

Indus AI ek AI-powered chat assistant hai jo Hindi aur English dono bhashaon mein sawaalon ke jawab deta hai. Ismein text chat, voice input/output, image generation, aur photo se solution jaisi cheezein shaamil hain.

Kya Indus AI free hai?

Haan, Indus AI free mein use kiya ja sakta hai, bina kisi sign-up ke. Login karna optional hai — sirf tab zaroori hai jab aap apni chat history ko cloud mein save rakhna chahte hain.

Kya mera data safe hai?

Login karke ki gayi chats securely cloud mein store hoti hain, aur sirf aap hi unhe dekh sakte hain. Guest mode mein data sirf aapke browser tak mehdood rehta hai.

Kaunsi bhashayein support hoti hain?

Abhi Indus AI Hindi aur English dono mein sahaj tareeke se baat kar sakta hai — jis bhasha mein aap poochenge, usi mein jawab milega.

Voice feature kaise use karein?
