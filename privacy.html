Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Indus AI ("we", "our") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our website and app.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account information: If you sign in, we store your email address (via Supabase authentication).
  • Chat data: Your questions and the AI's answers, so your conversation history can be shown back to you if you're signed in.
  • Uploaded photos: If you upload a photo (for example, to get help with a problem), it is sent to our AI provider for processing.
  • Technical data: General, non-identifying details like browser and device type, used only to keep the app running smoothly.

2. How We Use It

  • To generate the AI's responses to your questions
  • To save and sync your chat history (only if you're signed in)
  • To improve the app and fix issues

3. Third-Party Services

We rely on a small number of trusted services to run Indus AI:

  • AI providers (such as Google Gemini, and optionally others): to process your questions and generate responses
  • Supabase: for account authentication and storing chat data
  • Vercel: for hosting the website

Each of these services follows its own privacy policy.

4. Data Security

We use Row Level Security (RLS) so that only you can access your own conversations. No other user — even another signed-in user — can view your data.

5. Your Choices

  • You can sign out at any time from Settings
  • You can delete all of your chats at any time from Settings → "Delete all chats"
  • You can use Indus AI without an account (guest mode) — in that case, your data stays only in your own browser

6. Cookies and Local Storage

We use your browser's local storage to remember your preferences (like theme and accent color). This is not third-party tracking.

7. Children's Privacy

Indus AI is not intended for children under the age of 13.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Significant changes will be noted on this page.

9. Contact

For any questions, please reach out via Settings → Report a Problem inside the app.
