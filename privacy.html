Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Ye Privacy Policy batati hai ki Indus AI ("hum", "hamara") aapki jaankari kaise collect, use, aur protect karta hai jab aap hamari website ya app use karte hain.

1. Hum Kaunsi Jaankari Collect Karte Hain

  • Account jaankari: Agar aap login karte hain, hum aapka email address store karte hain (Supabase authentication ke through).
  • Chat data: Aapke sawaal aur AI ke jawab, taaki aapki chat history aapko dobara dikhayi ja sake (agar aap logged in hain).
  • Uploaded photos: Agar aap koi photo upload karte hain (jaise solution ke liye), wo AI provider ko process ke liye bheji jaati hai.
  • Technical data: Browser type, device type — general analytics ke liye, kisi vyakti ki pehchaan se jodne ke liye nahi.

2. Hum Iska Use Kaise Karte Hain

  • Aapko AI se jawab dene ke liye
  • Aapki chat history save aur sync karne ke liye (agar login kiya ho)
  • App ko behtar banane aur bugs theek karne ke liye

3. Third-Party Services

Hum kuch trusted third-party services use karte hain:

  • Google Gemini API — aapke sawaalon ko process karke AI jawab generate karne ke liye
  • Supabase — account authentication aur chat data store karne ke liye
  • Vercel — website hosting ke liye

Ye services apni khud ki privacy policies follow karti hain.

4. Data Security

Hum Row Level Security (RLS) jaisi techniques use karte hain taaki sirf aap hi apni chats dekh sakein. Koi bhi doosra user (chahe wo bhi logged in ho) aapka data nahi dekh sakta.

5. Aapke Rights

  • Aap apna account settings se logout kar sakte hain
  • Aap apni sabhi chats settings mein "Delete all chats" se hata sakte hain
  • Aap bina login ke bhi (guest mode) app use kar sakte hain — us surat mein data sirf aapke browser tak seemit rehta hai

6. Cookies aur Local Storage

Hum aapki preferences (jaise theme, accent color) yaad rakhne ke liye browser ke local storage ka use karte hain. Ye tracking cookies nahi hain.

7. Bachon Ki Privacy

Indus AI 13 saal se kam umar ke bachon ke liye nahi banaya gaya hai.

8. Policy Mein Badlaav

Hum is policy ko samay-samay par update kar sakte hain. Koi bada badlaav hone par is page par notice diya jayega.

9. Contact

Kisi bhi sawaal ke liye, app ke Settings → Report a Problem section se sampark karein.
